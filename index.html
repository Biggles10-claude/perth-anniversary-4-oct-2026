Soft guidance · top recommended pick

Romantic · Kings Park sunset + Madeleine dinner

Why: Sunday-open French bistro with strong independent reviews, walkable Highgate intimacy, and Frasers/6HEAD as alternate view dinners — no ferry risk, no Swan Valley driving fatigue. Soft pick only; Adventure and Chill-Luxury are equally complete below.
